# EcoRoute: Smart Waste Management & Garbage Routing System
## Final Viva Introduction

Good [Morning/Afternoon] respected examiners and teachers. 

My project is **EcoRoute**, a Smart Waste Management and Garbage Routing System. 

### What is the problem?
In our traditional garbage collection system, there are a few common problems:
- **For Citizens:** People don't know exactly when the garbage truck will arrive at their neighborhood.
- **For Admins (Municipality):** It is hard to track where the trucks are and whether they have finished their routes.
- **For Drivers:** They don't have a simple way to report when they have finished their job or if there is an issue.

### What is EcoRoute?
EcoRoute is a web-based platform that solves these problems by connecting everyone involved in the waste collection process. It provides real-time tracking, simple communication, and clear schedules.

### Key Features (The Three Portals)
Our system is divided into three main parts:

1. **The Admin Dashboard (For the Municipality):** 
   This allows administrators to monitor the garbage trucks in real-time. They can see which driver is assigned to which zone and track their progress live.

2. **The Driver App (For Truck Drivers):** 
   This is a very simple, easy-to-use mobile interface for drivers. With just one tap, they can mark their job as "Done" and update the admin immediately. It is specifically designed to be highly user-friendly for drivers on the go.

3. **The Citizen Portal (For House Owners):** 
   This portal helps citizens easily check their neighborhood's garbage collection schedule, exact pickup times, and guidelines on how to separate waste. 

### Technology Used
To build this platform, we used modern and fast web technologies:
- **Next.js & React:** For building the website.
- **Tailwind CSS:** For the responsive design.
- **Supabase:** As our backend database to manage real-time updates.

### Conclusion
In short, **EcoRoute** makes waste management smarter, more transparent, and much easier for the municipality, the drivers, and the citizens.
